Terms of Service — OpenProxy credits
The rules that govern the purchase of software credit plans, including the no-refund policy you accept at checkout.

1. What you buy

A plan grants a USD-denominated balance of consumable software credit. You spend that balance by making requests to the OpenProxy API; every request deducts its own cost from the plan balance. The credit is measured and metered by the software itself, and the balance and its deductions are visible to you in the dashboard under your key.

2. Immediate delivery

The credit activates automatically the moment your payment confirms on-chain. There is no waiting period, no manual provisioning and no deferred component: the full software service tied to the plan becomes usable at once. This is why the purchases below cannot be reversed.

3. No refunds

Credit is consumable software delivered in full upon activation. Purchases cannot be cancelled, reversed or refunded once the payment confirms, and unused balance is not returned when it expires with the plan. Before you pay, the checkout shows every fact that matters: the plan price, the credited balance, the models included and the exact expiry date. If you do not accept these conditions, do not complete the purchase.

  • Prices are per plan, never per request.
  • Unused balance does not carry over past the plan's expiry date.
  • A payment that arrives after the price window closes but within the confirmation grace still credits the plan; one that arrives later is not credited and is not owed a plan.

4. Payment

Payments are made in USDC on the Base network, for the exact amount shown at checkout. The price window is quoted before you send the transfer; the page updates by itself while the transfer confirms on-chain.

5. Key custody

The API key that receives the credit is shown once and cannot be recovered by OpenProxy. Store it safely. A key lost by its owner cannot be re-issued, and its remaining balance cannot be transferred.

6. Acceptance

Checking the Terms of Service box during checkout constitutes your acceptance of these terms. The version in force at your purchase is the one published at /terms at that moment (version below).
