FAQ
What am I buying? Software credit: a plan that grants a USD-denominated balance you consume through the OpenProxy API. The balance is live the moment your payment confirms on-chain and it expires on the plan's date.
Are prices per request? No. You buy a plan once; every request deducts its own cost from that plan's balance. The price on the card is the plan price, not a per-call fee.
Can I cancel or get a refund? No. Credit is consumable software delivered in full the moment the payment confirms, so purchases cannot be cancelled, reversed or refunded (see the Terms of Service).
Where do I see what I have left? Open the “My key” tab, paste your key and press “Load my plan”: it shows the plan name, the balance left and the expiry date.
What if my payment is late? A transfer sent before the deadline still credits the plan when it confirms on-chain, even if the timer expired. Keep the tab open: the page resumes by itself.
